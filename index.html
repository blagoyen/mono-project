03 — FAQ

Good
questions.

Still curious? The setup guide covers the technical parts.

Which sites are supported?

Anything yt-dlp supports — YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X, Facebook, Vimeo, SoundCloud, Reddit, Twitch, Dailymotion and more than a thousand others. Playlists are intentionally ignored: one link, one file.

Is it really free?

Yes. The project is open source and runs locally. There is no account, no payment and no advertising — the only cost is the bandwidth of the machine running it.

What quality should I choose?

For archiving, pick the highest MP4 resolution the source offers. For sharing, 1080p is the sweet spot. For music and podcasts, 320 kbps MP3 is effectively transparent.

Where do my files go?

They are written to a temporary folder on the server, then streamed to your browser and deleted automatically after one hour. Nothing is uploaded anywhere else.

Can I download private or age-restricted videos?

No. Only public content that is accessible without an account is supported. Live streams and paid content are not available either.

Is this legal?

Download only what you own or have permission to save. Downloading copyrighted material can violate a platform’s terms of service and local law. You are responsible for how you use it.
